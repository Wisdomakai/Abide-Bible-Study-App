Community Guidelines — Ardent

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Ardent groups are places to study, reflect, and pray together. Everything you post to a group — text and voice — must follow these guidelines. Posting in a group means you agree to them.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
  • Hateful or demeaning content about a person or group, including because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, disability, or age.
  • Sexual or sexually suggestive content, and any content that sexualises minors.
  • Graphic violence, or promotion of violent or illegal activity.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, or misleading links.
  • Sharing someone else's private information, or another person's private prayer request, without their permission.
  • Impersonating another person.

Reporting and blocking

On any group post from someone else, tap ⋯ to Report post or Block that member. Reports are private: the person you report is not told who reported them. Blocking hides that member's posts from you; you can unblock them in Settings.

What we do

We review every report within 24 hours. Posts that break these guidelines are removed, and members who break them — especially repeatedly — may lose access to Ardent. Zero tolerance applies to content that sexualises minors or threatens violence, which may also be reported to the authorities.

Contact
