We reply within two working days. Reports of harmful content are reviewed within 24 hours.

Getting started

Open Ardent, tap Get started and enter the name your group will see. No email or password is needed to begin.

Joining or creating a group

Go to the Group tab and tap Create or join a group. To join, enter the invite code someone shared with you. To create one, give it a name and share the invite code with your group.

Keeping your journal safe

While you use Ardent as a guest, your journal is tied to this phone. To keep it if you change or reset your phone, open Settings, add your email under Save your account, confirm it from the email we send, then choose a password. You can then sign in on any device.

Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Open the link we send on the same phone to choose a new password.

Reporting or blocking someone

On any group post from someone else, tap ⋯ to report the post or block that member. Blocked members are listed in Settings, where you can unblock them. See our Community Guidelines.

Deleting your account

Open Settings and tap Delete account and data. This permanently deletes your account, journal, notes, prayers, group posts and recordings. If you can't open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Daily reminder

Turn on Remind me to reflect in Settings and pick a time. If the reminder doesn't appear, check that notifications are allowed for Ardent in your phone's settings.
