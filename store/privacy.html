Privacy Policy — Ardent

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Ardent ("the app", "we", "us") is a journaling app for Bible study groups. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. By using Ardent you agree to this policy.

Information we handle

  • Your email and name — used for secure passwordless sign-in, journal sync, and identifying posts you choose to share.
  • Personal journal, reflections, notes, and prayers — encrypted in transit and stored in your private Supabase account row so they remain available across your devices.
  • Group posts — reflections, notes, and prayers you choose to share, plus "Amen" reactions, are stored on our server so your group members can see them.
  • Voice recordings — recordings you add are stored in a private bucket. Group members receive temporary signed access only when you share the recording in their group.
  • Web Push subscription and reminder preferences — stored only after you explicitly enable notifications, including your browser push endpoint, timezone, and chosen reminder time.
  • Account activity — recent sign-in/presence times are retained for security and aggregate service administration.

How we use it

Solely to provide the app's features: showing your group's shared feed, sending the notifications you opt into, and syncing your private journal across your devices. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising or profiling.

Service providers

  • Supabase — stores the shared group feed and accounts. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Your browser/device push service — delivers Web Push notifications after you opt in.
  • bible-api.com — supplies verse text (KJV); only the verse reference is requested.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your own group posts in the app at any time. Deleting a journal note or prayer also removes every shared copy and its owned recording before the local item is removed. Settings includes a self-service “Delete account and data” action that permanently deletes the account, journal, memberships, posts, reactions, subscriptions, and owned voice files.

Children

Ardent is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new date.

Your choices

You can disable browser notifications in your device settings, sign out without deleting data, or permanently remove your account and data from Ardent Settings.
